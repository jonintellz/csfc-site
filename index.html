Who we are

Built for the voters nobody else is talking to

Most political money goes to people who have already made up their minds. We spend on the ones who have not.

Common Sense for California runs independent voter programs in the districts where the margin is thin. We start from the voter file, not a media plan: we identify the households that are genuinely undecided, reach them directly, test what moves them, and stay with them through the day their ballot is counted.

We are organized as an independent expenditure committee under federal law. That lets us accept contributions of any amount from individuals, corporations, partnerships and other organizations, and it obliges us to report every dollar in and every dollar out to the Federal Election Commission.

How we work

Precision, not volume

We do not carpet a district with advertising and hope. We identify the households that decide the outcome and reach them directly, in the order the calendar demands.

Find
The undecided voters in a close district, identified from the voter file, household by household.
Reach
Direct conversations, connected television and streaming video, matched to those households by name, never bought by zip code.
Deliver
Stay with every voter through the day the ballot is counted, and report every dollar to the Federal Election Commission.

Why California

The road to a common sense majority runs through California

No state sends more members to Congress, and no state has more seats decided by a few thousand votes. Winning California's close districts is how the country gets a Congress that reflects it.
