Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Common Sense for California collects through this website (commonsensecaliforniapac.com) and how it is used. Common Sense for California is a federal independent-expenditure political committee (FEC ID C00942920).

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you sign up for text messages we collect your first name, mobile phone number and, if you provide it, your ZIP code. If you contribute through our contribution page, our payment processor collects the details federal law requires (name, address, occupation and employer).

Information collected automatically. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and roughly where visitors are. Google Analytics uses cookies. You can block cookies in your browser or install Google's opt-out add-on.

How we use it

We use your mobile number only to send you the text messages you signed up for. We use contribution information to process contributions and to report them as federal election law requires. We use analytics information to improve the site.

Sharing

No mobile opt-in information will be shared with third parties.

We do not sell personal information. Contributor information is reported to the Federal Election Commission when the law requires it, and those reports are public.

Text messaging

Messaging Terms and Conditions: You agree to receive updates, donation asks, and informational messages from Common Sense for California. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. For help, reply HELP or email us at info@commonsensecaliforniapac.com. You can opt-out at any time by replying STOP.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving messages. To ask us to delete the mobile number and name you gave us, email info@commonsensecaliforniapac.com.
